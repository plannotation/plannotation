# Plannotation samples

Generated by `make samples` (or `plannotation samples build`) from three seeded IFC4
models. Nothing here but this file is committed: every build writes the same bytes,
so the drawings cannot drift from the code that makes them.

Each directory holds `model.ifc`, `sheet.svg`, `sheet.pdf`, `sheet.labelled.pdf`,
`labels.json` and `groundtruth.jsonl`; `groundtruth.jsonl` beside them merges all
three. Every labelled sheet validates at conformance level **L3** with no errors and
no warnings, cross-checked against its model, and rasterises identically to its
unlabelled PDF. The callouts form a cycle, so the three are one set of drawings.

## `floorplan` — ARC-101, Grundriss Erdgeschoss, 1:50

An 8.0 × 6.0 m single-storey house on the grid A–D / 1–4, cut at 1.20 m.

- Five walls (`Pos. 1`–`Pos. 5`): four 240 mm external walls and a 115 mm internal
  wall on grid C.
- Two doors (`T1`, `T2`) and two windows (`W1`, `W2`), each in an opening cut into
  its wall.
- Eight dimensions: the bays A–B–C–D and 1–2–3–4, and the two overall lengths, each
  linked to the grids it runs between.
- A callout to TWP-201.

## `positionsplan` — TWP-201, Positionsplan Gründung, 1:50

A foundation position plan on the grid A–C / 1–3, cut at 0.50 m.

- Nine 30/30 columns (`Pos. 1`–`Pos. 9`) and six 30/75 ground beams
  (`Pos. 10`–`Pos. 15`), cut; a 25 cm base slab (`Pos. 16`) below the cut, drawn as
  a projection.
- Every member's cross-section printed under its mark, from the model's
  `Pset_*Common.Reference`, and linked to that property in the label.
- Six dimensions, bays and overall, and a callout to ARC-301.

## `section` — ARC-301, Schnitt A-A, 1:50

A vertical section across a two-storey house, looking along x, grids 1–4.

- Walls on both storeys (`Pos. 1`–`Pos. 4`) and three slabs (`Pos. 5`–`Pos. 7`),
  all cut.
- Level marks at ±0,00, +3,00 and +6,00, each linked to its storey's GlobalId and
  placed so that it stands at the elevation it prints.
- Seven dimensions: the grid bays and overall, and the storey heights and overall
  height, which measure between the levels.
- A callout to ARC-101.

## Ground truth

`groundtruth.jsonl` asks questions whose answers come from the model, never from
reading the drawing: counts per IFC class, the scale and drawing type, every
dimension, the callout's target, the grid axes, what a mark names, a member's
cross-section, a level's elevation, and — marked `requiresLabel` — a GlobalId, which
no drawing prints. The benchmark asks them (`bench/questions.jsonl`).
